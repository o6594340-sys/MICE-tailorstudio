# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML, CSS and browser JavaScript were selected in the approved implementation plan because the approved presentation needs one fast, portable route without application state.

## Users

Primary readers are decision-makers evaluating a concept for a November 2026 TNPRO contractors’ offsite near Moscow. The immediate job is to understand the event’s narrative, tone and intended guest experience in seven screens.

## Product Purpose

This is a concise concept presentation, not a general Granat club site, ticketing page or confirmed event programme. Success means the reader understands «Запас прочности» as professional experience, a trusted circle and time to recover.

## Positioning

The presentation frames strength as a durable professional resource shared among equals, rather than as a physical challenge or generic corporate entertainment.

## Operating Context

The page is reviewed as a premium client-facing event concept on desktop and mobile. It precedes final selection of venue, programme mechanics and budget.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One responsive route with exactly seven screens from the approved TЗ.
- No forms, registration, CMS, storage or third-party integrations.
- Text remains separate from layout and is not augmented with invented claims.
- SPA, fire, bar and activity mechanics remain conditional on venue and production choices.

## Brand Commitments

Use the Granat TNPRO lookbook supplied in the sibling project folder: deep black only as a framing colour, corporate red `#B1000B`, red-pink gradient, approved Granat mark and TNPRO × Granat co-brand hierarchy. The people are self-made, energetic, relatively young working professionals: the visual tone must be warm, clear, active and human, never old-money, cigar-lounge luxury or an exclusive-nightclub fantasy. Exclude literal pomegranates outside the mark, Oriental decor, gold, concrete, camouflage and banal corporate-banquet imagery.

## Product Principles

1. Show a directed emotional arc, not a timetable.
2. Let self-made professional equality, movement and chosen pace lead over spectacle.
3. Use warm light and real human energy to prove the concept while preserving production honesty.
4. Make the site usable and complete at a mobile viewport as well as on a large desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The presentation requires semantic sections, readable Russian copy, keyboard focus, contrast-safe text, a skip link, and a reduced-motion experience.
