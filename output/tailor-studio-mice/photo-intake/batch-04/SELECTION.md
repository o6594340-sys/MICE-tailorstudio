## Missing food layer to add later

1. one refined plated main course with a regional Chinese character;

These should match the real photography in light and texture: warm natural restaurant light, believable portions, tactile tableware and no exaggerated luxury styling. If generated, they must be treated as illustrative editorial imagery, never as evidence of a venue, supplier or completed event.
