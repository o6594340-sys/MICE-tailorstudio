# Design System: China in Motion

## Creative North Star

An international travel editorial experienced as a moving sequence rather than a conventional agency landing page. China feels contemporary, human and varied. Photography leads; typography controls pace; mandarin orange supplies moments of energy.

## First Viewport

“China, in layers.” opens the page as a composition, not a single destination postcard. On desktop, the title and introduction occupy the left reading field while an asymmetric, real-photo collage holds the right: magnolia over a classical Suzhou garden, a boat in a willow-lined canal, and a small butterfly-embroidery detail. The three scenes establish garden, water and handwork without implying a completed project or venue.

On mobile, the title and copy lead above the collage. The same three-photo relationship remains legible through overlapping plates and generous breathing room; the embroidery detail stays a small tactile interruption rather than becoming a fourth text block.

## Navigation

A quiet fixed header shows the current chapter, contact action and Menu. Menu opens into a full-screen ink-blue editorial index with one visual field that changes by section. Navigation stays obvious and keyboard accessible; Escape closes the overlay.

## Layout and Rhythm

The page alternates full-bleed moments with generous warm-white reading space. Photo ratios vary intentionally: wide hero, tall editorial portraits, human details, large destination stage and panoramic travel pause. Same-size card grids are not used.

## Photography

Real, candid and lightly imperfect photography is the norm. Supplied photographs lead across documentary life, transport, hospitality, venues, craft and city scale. Two selective AI food still lifes act only as editorial pauses, remain visibly labelled as illustrative, and never stand in for a real venue or completed project.

Display derivatives are gently normalised with non-destructive day, night and indoor treatments. The originals remain untouched in the intake archive.

## Interaction

- The fixed header reports the visible chapter.
- Full-screen menu imagery responds to navigation focus or hover.
- Programme formats update one shared visual preview.
- Destination tabs update one large editorial stage and its concise programme thought.
- Motion is brief and optional; reduced-motion preferences disable it.

## Voice

Clear, warm, internationally legible English. No inflated scale claims, faux luxury language, completed-project implications or generic “unforgettable experiences” copy. Destination sections are explicitly programme inspiration.

## Responsive Behaviour

Desktop uses asymmetric editorial spreads. The hero's copy and scene are deliberately separated into left reading space and right collage space; its plates vary in scale and overlap rather than resolving into a card grid. Mobile preserves the sequence, places the hero copy above the collage, gives images meaningful height and turns the menu into a single readable column. Contact details and WeChat remain immediately usable.
